## 1. Overall Website Aim

The website should feel **modern, friendly, trustworthy and professional**, while still being approachable for both students and parents.

The main purpose of the website is to:

* Explain who we are and why we tutor.
* Clearly explain what tutoring we offer.
* Show the subjects and year groups we cover.
* Make our personalised approach stand out.
* Give parents/students a clear idea of pricing.
* Build trust through reviews and information about us.
* Make it extremely easy to enquire or book a session.

### Overall design style

I like the general feel of the **Explore Learning** website, particularly the use of **cards, sections and clear calls-to-action**.

Reference:
https://www.explorelearning.co.uk/

However, I don't want to copy their website. Instead, use the idea of:

* Large rounded cards
* Clear sections
* Friendly illustrations/photos
* Plenty of white space
* Short blocks of text rather than huge paragraphs
* Icons to represent different benefits
* Strong CTA buttons
* Subtle animations when scrolling
* A modern, premium but approachable tutoring aesthetic

The site should work perfectly on **desktop, tablet and mobile**.

## 6. Our Tutoring

This should be one of the main sections of the website.

Heading:

**Tutoring Designed Around You**

Short introduction explaining that every student is different and sessions are adapted accordingly.

Then use **four large feature cards**.

### Card 1 — Personalised

#### Tutoring built around the individual

We start by understanding where the student currently is, what they find difficult and what they want to achieve.

Sessions are then tailored accordingly.

### Card 2 — Understanding

#### Learn it. Understand it. Apply it.

Rather than simply teaching students how to memorise answers, we focus on developing genuine understanding.

### Card 3 — Confidence

#### A space where students can ask questions

Students should feel comfortable saying:

"I don't understand."

We want tutoring sessions to be supportive and encouraging, rather than intimidating.

### Card 4 — Progress

#### Work towards clear goals

Sessions should have a purpose.

We identify weaknesses, work on them and track progress over time.

## 7. How Sessions Work

Create a horizontal 3 or 4-step process.

### Step 1 — Understand

We start by understanding the student's current level, strengths, weaknesses and goals.

### Step 2 — Personalise

We create sessions around the student's specific needs.

### Step 3 — Learn

Lessons combine explanation, examples, guided practice and independent questions.

### Step 4 — Progress

We review progress and adapt future sessions accordingly.

Visually this could be represented as:

**01 → 02 → 03 → 04**

with connecting lines between each step.

## 8. What a Session Looks Like

**01 — Recap**

## 21. Design / Visual Style

I want the site to feel:

**Modern + Friendly + Premium + Academic + Approachable**

Avoid making it look like a traditional school website.

### Colours

We can choose a colour palette, but ideally:

* Light/white background
* One strong primary colour
* One complementary accent colour
* Dark text
* Very light backgrounds for alternating sections

For example, we could use a deep navy/blue as the main colour with a lighter accent colour.

Don't make every section heavily coloured.

Use colour strategically for:

* Buttons
* Icons
* Cards
* Highlights
* Statistics
* CTA sections

## 31. The Overall User Journey

The website should be designed around a parent landing on the site and thinking:

### "Can these tutors help my child?"

They should quickly find:

**WHO YOU ARE**

→ About Us

**WHAT YOU DO**

→ Our Tutoring

**WHETHER YOU COVER THEIR NEED**

→ Subjects + Year Groups

**HOW MUCH IT COSTS**

→ Prices

**WHETHER YOU ARE GOOD**

→ Reviews + Results

**WHAT THEY SHOULD DO NEXT**

→ Book a Session / Contact Us

So the website shouldn't feel like a huge amount of information that the visitor has to work through.

It should progressively answer their questions and then encourage them to enquire.

---

## 32. Key Design Idea

The biggest thing I'd like the website to capture is:

### "Personal tutoring, not generic tutoring."

We should make this the central message throughout the website.

Instead of just saying:

**"We offer tutoring in Maths and English."**

The website should communicate:

**"We get to know the student, identify what they need, and build sessions around them."**

That should be reflected visually through the personalised cards, the "How It Works" process, testimonials and the enquiry form.

The site should make parents feel:

**"My child won't just be another student — these tutors will actually understand what they need."**

### Phase 1 — Essential

* Navigation
* Hero
* About Us
* Our Tutoring
* Subjects
* Prices
* Reviews
* Contact form
* Footer
* Mobile responsiveness

### Phase 2 — Nice to have

* Subject filtering
* Review carousel
* FAQ accordion
* Animated statistics
* More sophisticated animations
* 10-session package purchasing

* Online booking system
* Online payments
* Student login area
* Progress dashboard

The initial website should therefore be **simple enough to launch quickly but designed so these features can be added later.**
